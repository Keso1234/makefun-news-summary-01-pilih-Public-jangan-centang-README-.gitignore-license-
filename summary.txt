World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026: Ukraine-Russia-US talks in Abu Dhabi ended after two days with a prisoner exchange agreed in principle but no breakthrough on territory, which US envoy Jared Kushner called the hardest issue (Meduza). Iran designated Argentina's navy and air force terrorist organisations (Iran Foreign Ministry), and the US Justice Department charged three Iran-backed operatives over about 20 plots in the US and Europe, including a September plot at RAF Fairford (BBC). Israeli forces killed at least seven people in Gaza on Tuesday, including a father and his 13-year-old son (Just Security). Israel's new Knesset was sworn in with no government after Benjamin Netanyahu lost his majority, and Gadi Eisenkot was tapped to try to form one (Zark News). At COP31 in Antalya, UN chief Antonio Guterres told leaders the slow climate response put the world on a path to destruction; 102 heads of state gathered for the summit (Just Security). The US House voted 211-204 to refer the impeachment resolution against Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth to the Judiciary Committee, effectively killing it (Rest of the World Report). Congo's Ebola outbreak reached 11,042 cases and at least 5,387 deaths, government data showed, the second-largest and deadliest on record (Rest of the World Report). Emmanuel and Brigitte Macron filed a new defamation suit against US podcaster Candace Owens in a Tennessee federal court (Rest of the World Report).
